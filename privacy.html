Privacy Policy — CheckGate

Effective Date: November 2026

CheckGate ("the App") is built 100% on Atlassian Forge and operates exclusively within Atlassian's cloud infrastructure.

1. Zero External Data Egress

CheckGate does not transmit, copy, or store any Jira issue data, project templates, or user information on any external or third-party servers.

2. Data Stored Inside Jira

Checklist items and completion timestamps (including display name for audit trail) are stored strictly within your Jira instance (Jira Issue Properties and Forge Key-Value Storage).

3. Data Deletion

Uninstalling the App removes access immediately, and issue properties remain solely within your Atlassian site.
